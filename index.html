About

Alex Rivers is a saxophonist with over two decades of experience performing across jazz clubs, concert halls, and studio sessions. Known for a warm, expressive tone and a deep command of both traditional and contemporary styles, Alex has shared the stage with acclaimed ensembles and released recordings spanning bebop, soul-jazz, and modern fusion.

Alongside performing, Alex teaches saxophone to students of all levels — from complete beginners picking up the horn for the first time to advanced players refining their improvisation. Lessons are available in person and online.
